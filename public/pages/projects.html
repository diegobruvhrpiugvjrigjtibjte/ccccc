Selected Projects at a Glance

  • Highly dissipating carbon nanotube nanocomposites
  • NiTiNOL/steel wire rope assemblies for advanced structures
  • Flutter control of suspension bridges by hysteretic mass dampers
  • Ultrasonic delamination detection for composite structures

Research Topics (Selected)

  • Nonlinear dynamics and stability: nonlinear reduced modeling/phenomena in macro/micro structures and mechanical systems
  • Nonlinear multi-scale vibration absorbers: development of innovative vibration absorbers for cranes, bridges, wind turbines, and micro applications.
  • Nanocomposites: high-damping, high-strength carbon nanotube composites
  • Nonlinear multi-scale dynamics for design: resonances/energy transfers/multistability/bifurcations in sensors/actuators/devices
  • Smart materials and devices: NiTiNOL-steel strands, tensility structures
  • Experimental nonlinear dynamics: nonlinear frequency response analysis of rods, plates, structures and systems

Laboratory & Granted Patents

The listed lab is devoted to undergraduate, graduate, and post-graduate activities.
