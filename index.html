Official Invitation

New Design Paradigms

Funded through an AFOSR London Office project at the University of RomaTre, Dr. "Les" Lee presents this workshop as part of the M4 program. Following multiple rescheduling due to global restrictions, we are finally bringing this scientific excellence to the heart of Rome.

This registration-free workshop explores how AI, Machine Learning, and Quantum Computing are reshaping the discovery of materials with integrated mechanical, thermal, and electrical functionalities for extreme environments.

AFOSR
